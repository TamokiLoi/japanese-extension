# Kanji book mnemonic source data

This app adds source-specific mnemonic choices from the three books supplied in
`assets/FPT-N3/kanji-look-and-learn/`. Each choice records its book, item
number, source edition, and PDF page. For N5–N4, illustrations use the original
book while mnemonics use the Vietnamese scanned companion. The app includes 510
N5–N4 entries, 356 N2–N1 entries, and 327 N3–N2 entries in the lazy-loaded
excerpt data. Separate checked paraphrases and visual notes cover the 15 kanji
that lacked an app mnemonic.

N5–N4 tips are labeled as OCR or as checked directly against the scanned page.
Most Vietnamese scan tips are OCR transcriptions and may contain recognition
errors; compare them with their source page when needed. The original N5–N4
compound vocabulary is not included because its OCR layer does not reliably
separate Japanese words, readings, and English glosses. The Vietnamese source
wording is retained for the other two books.

The books' redistribution rights have not been verified. Keep this source data
in the user's private workspace until the right to publish it with the app has
been confirmed.
